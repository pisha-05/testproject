# LAUNCH STRATEGY & GO-TO-MARKET: ACCESSROUTE LIVE™

## 1. Product Positioning & Value Narrative
Standard navigation systems solve for the fastest car drive. AccessRoute Live solves for the **real-world physical journey** of pedestrians, wheelchair users, visually impaired travelers, parents with strollers, and elderly citizens.

### Value Pillars:
1. **100% Step-Free Routing**: Hard constraints eliminate unexpected staircases and steep inclines (>5%).
2. **Community Verified Freshness**: Crowdsourced obstacle reports with upvote confirmation and automatic decay prevent "phantom elevators" and outdated construction traps.
3. **Live Detour Intelligence**: In-trip obstacle detection proactively reroutes you around newly formed barricades or broken ramps.
4. **WCAG AAA Compliance**: Built from the ground up for high contrast, keyboard navigation, screen readers, and directional speech synthesis.

---

## 2. Launch Checklist & Pilot Areas
- **Pilot Cities**: New Delhi (Connaught Place & Central Corridor), San Francisco (Market St & Embarcadero), London (King's Cross & West End).
- **Target Channels**: Disability advocacy organizations, accessibility councils, transit authorities, stroller parent groups.
- **Key Demo Flow**:
  1. Open map -> Select destination (e.g. Connaught Place Metro).
  2. Select **Wheelchair** mobility mode -> Observe 3 verified ramps & 0 stairs.
  3. Compare route alternatives -> Observe tradeoff disclosure.
  4. Click **Start Navigation** -> Observe turn-by-turn guidance and voice cues.
  5. Simulate barrier -> Observe instant detour recalculation corridor.
  6. Submit & vote on community hazard reports.
